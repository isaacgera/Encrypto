Encrypto

One-page overview · v1.0.0
Category: Utility Complexity: Medium Local-first PWA Zero network

What it is

A private, locally-encrypted wallet for you and your family — hold key document details (passport, licence, policy numbers), passwords/logins, card details and secure notes in one place. Everything is encrypted at rest in your browser and unlocked with a single master passphrase. No accounts, no servers, nothing ever leaves the device. An encrypted export/import gives you a safe backup.

Who it's for

Anyone who keeps important reference details scattered across notes apps, photos and memory — and wants them in one safe, searchable place. Built for personal and family use: the kind of details you occasionally need but shouldn't leave lying around in plain text.

Why it's useful

Most "quick places" to stash a card number or policy reference (notes, screenshots, messages) store it in plain text that anything on the device can read. Encrypto encrypts your data with strong, standard cryptography (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM via the browser's native Web Crypto), so what's stored on disk is just ciphertext — useless to anyone without your passphrase.

How it helps

Set a master passphrase once, then add entries by type (Document, Password, Card, Note). Search instantly, reveal or copy a value when you need it (the clipboard auto-clears after 30 seconds), and the vault auto-locks after 5 minutes idle or when you switch away. Install it to your home screen and it works fully offline.

The honest trade-off

There is no recovery. Your passphrase is the only key — forget it and the data is permanently unreadable, by design (no reset, no backdoor). Your vault also lives only in this browser on this device, so clearing browser data erases it: keep an encrypted export as backup. Encrypto protects your data at rest; it is not a hardware vault and can't protect a compromised device or a weak passphrase.
